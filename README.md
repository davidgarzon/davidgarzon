## What Production Taught Me

Shipped a multi-agent customer service system. Offline evals said **82% acceptance** against our full conversation history. Two days into production, **5% of responses were hallucinating** in front of real customers.

We had humans reviewing. But they reviewed *after* the response had already been sent. That is not a human in the loop. It is an autopsy.

Rebuilt in **15 days with 2 people**. One structural change: the agent drafts, a person owns what ships. Now resolving **75-78% of conversations**, with 70% of repetitive volume gone.

- Offline evals against past conversations do not predict production. Real conversations are open-ended.
- In customer-facing AI, 95% is a failing grade. Below ~98% you route to a human.
- We hit 75-78% *because* we never removed the people.

Intelligence is not an endpoint.

AI-native companies are designed around:
